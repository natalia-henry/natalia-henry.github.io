I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Political Science at the University of Pennsylvania.

My research lies at the intersection of international security and comparative politics, with a substantive focus on the political economy of conflict. I primarily explore questions related to military technology and innovation and battlefield effectiveness. I use a variety of quantitative and qualitative methods, including causal inference, natural-language processing, survey experiments, archival data collection, and qualitative interviews.

Prior to attending UPenn, I worked at the Boston Consulting Group (BCG), then as a consultant for the Office of Personnel Management (OPM). I received my BA from Dartmouth College in 2021, with a major in History and a minor in Government.

When not working, I'm probably running on roads or trails, planning my next travels, or in search of the best local hazy IPA.

I can be reached at nathenry@sas.upenn.edu.
